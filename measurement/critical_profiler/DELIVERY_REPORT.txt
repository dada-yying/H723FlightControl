Measurement semantics (do not conflate):
- body_cycles: sample after real port enter to sample before real port exit. This is a continuously masked sampled body interval including profiler/nested overhead. It excludes boundary instructions and is NOT an exact full BASEPRI assertion-to-release duration.
- envelope_cycles: before enter wrapper to after real port exit. May include task/ISR preemption before raising or after releasing BASEPRI. It is a conservative envelope, NOT an IRQ-disabled duration.
- outer_body/envelope maxima only update for the frame whose depth transitions 1->0. Nested intervals never restart the outer start. Each site's inclusive maximum includes its children.
- enter/exit envelope overhead and enter/exit bookkeeping maxima are recorded separately. They overlap body/envelope as documented; do not sum maxima or subtract a guessed overhead to fabricate an uninstrumented duration.
- A separate short unwrapped accounting critical section serializes counters after the application mask releases. Its sampled body maximum is reported separately, including when nested within a parent interval.
- Snapshot copy uses scheduler suspension rather than a long interrupt mask. Its sampled scheduling envelope is separate. This is measurement perturbation, not production behavior.
- Integer cycles are retained exactly as uint32. Microseconds are ceil(cycles/(clock/1e6)); non-integer-MHz clocks reject the run. No floating-point printf or dynamic allocation is used by the profiler.

Resource disclosure:
Cp_Exit was reduced from 88 to 56 static stack bytes. Measured-build RcTask static call-chain depth is 504 bytes, versus 456 in the production build. Its original 512-byte allocation leaves insufficient exception/context margin.
Therefore the measurement-only Cp_ThreadNew adapter allocates RcTask 1024 bytes (+512 from the existing FreeRTOS heap). It preserves entry, argument, priority and name. No production task attributes/source are edited; other tasks are unchanged. Snapshot measurement_rc_stack_bytes=1024 discloses this.
Static linker depth is not a runtime stack guarantee; verify all task high-water marks and heap after startup on the board. No hidden stack-setting change is applied to production.

Output:
Use g_cp_snapshot in Keil Watch/memory export. catalog maps IDs to source/function/entry. complete and sequence must remain stable across a read. valid=0 or any fault rejects the entire run; hits=0 is NOT MEASURED.
Snapshot refresh occurs on the existing 100 Hz telemetry call site. Cp_TelemetryTap invokes original VOFA_SendFrame with exactly the original frame and returns its original HAL status. No profiler channel/bytes are appended and there is NO measurement stream on UART in this implementation. Thus there is no mixed-format serial stream or lossy float cycle encoding.
A frozen/nonadvancing sequence is not evidence of a safe duration: discard halted/deadlocked runs. Missing exits can stop scheduling and prevent a new snapshot; this is detected operationally by missing heartbeat, not a fabricated timing value.

Invalid runs:
Sticky depth overflow, unmatched entry/exit, invalid site, ISR use, unexpected masked outer entry, task-owner mismatch, disabled/changed clock, zero or >1 s sampled interval, counter overflow and snapshot inside an active mask invalidate capture. Reboot starts a new run; no runtime clear/reset hides faults. Startup pairs are balanced through the original primitives but excluded from timing.
Unsigned subtraction supports one CYCCNT wrap. Multiple wraps combined with halted HAL tick cannot be diagnosed from these clocks alone. Do not halt the core during a run, enter sleep, or trust a run with a stopped snapshot heartbeat. HardFault/NMI/terminal fault runs are outside acceptance.
